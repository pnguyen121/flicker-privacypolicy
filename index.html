Privacy Policy for Flicker

Last Updated: January 12, 2026

Summary (Quick Reference)

  • ✅ What we access: Your photo library (on-device only)
  • ✅ What we collect: Anonymous usage analytics via Firebase
  • ❌ What we DON'T do: Upload your photos, sell your data, or track you personally
  • ✅ Your control: Full control via iOS Settings and in-app options
  • ✅ Security: Industry-standard encryption and security practices

Flicker is designed with privacy in mind. Your photos are yours and stay on your device.

Introduction

Flicker ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Flicker mobile application (the "App").

Information We Collect

1. Photo Library Access

Flicker requires access to your photo library to provide its core functionality:

  • What we access: Photos and videos stored on your device
  • How we use it: To display your photos for organization and allow you to delete unwanted photos
  • Storage: All photo data remains on your device. We do not upload, store, or transmit your photos to our servers
  • Control: You can revoke photo library access at any time in your device Settings

2. Push Notifications

With your permission, we send push notifications:

  • Purpose: Daily reminders to view memories from past years ("On This Day" feature)
  • Frequency: Maximum one notification per day, only when you have memories to view
  • Control: You can disable notifications at any time in your device Settings or within the App

3. Analytics Data

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with our App:

Data collected:
  • Feature usage (which features you use most often)
  • App interactions (buttons clicked, screens viewed)
  • Session duration and frequency
  • Device type and operating system version
  • General location (country/region level only)
  • Crash reports and performance data
Data NOT collected:
  • Your name, email, or contact information
  • Your photos or photo metadata
  • Precise location data
  • Any personally identifiable information (PII)
  • Purpose: To improve the App, fix bugs, and understand which features are most valuable to users
  • Provider: Google Firebase (subject to Google's Privacy Policy)

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  1. Core Functionality: Access photos to enable you to organize and delete unwanted photos
  2. App Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and user experience
  3. Bug Fixes: Identify and resolve technical issues
  4. Feature Development: Understand which features to prioritize

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • All your photos remain on your device
  • Swipe session data (your keep/delete decisions) is stored locally on your device
  • App settings and preferences are stored locally using iOS UserDefaults

Cloud Storage

  • We do NOT upload or store your photos on our servers
  • Analytics data is transmitted to Firebase servers (encrypted in transit)
  • No backup of your personal data is stored in our cloud infrastructure

Security Measures

  • All data transmission uses industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS)
  • We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access
  • Analytics data is anonymized and cannot be traced back to you personally

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We Do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your photos with anyone
  • Use your data for advertising purposes
  • Share your data with data brokers

We DO share anonymized analytics data with:

  • Firebase/Google: For analytics and crash reporting services only
  • This data is aggregated and cannot identify you personally

Legal Requirements:

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights:

1. Access Control:

  • Revoke photo library access at any time (Settings → Privacy → Photos)
  • Disable notifications (Settings → Notifications → Flicker)

2. Data Deletion:

  • All app data is stored locally on your device
  • Deleting the App removes all associated data
  • Analytics data is retained by Firebase according to their retention policy

3. Opt-Out:

  • You can choose not to grant photo library access (App will not function)
  • You can decline notification permissions (App will still function)

Children's Privacy

Flicker does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App is intended for general audiences. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App, please contact us.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  1. Firebase Analytics (Google LLC)
    • Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
    • Purpose: Analytics and crash reporting
  2. iOS PhotoKit (Apple Inc.)
    • Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
    • Purpose: Photo library access

Data Retention

  • Photos: Never stored by us; remain on your device only
  • App Settings: Stored locally until you delete the App
  • Analytics Data: Retained by Firebase for up to 2 months (rolling window)

Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

European Users (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under GDPR:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of data we have about you
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to Object: Object to data processing

Note: Since photos remain on your device and analytics data is anonymized, we have minimal identifiable data about you.
